# Third-party notices

## Research and bibliography

`bibliography/references.bib` contains bibliographic metadata for books,
papers, and other research sources. It does not redistribute those works.
Contributors must preserve source attribution and use the existing BibTeX
keys when citing material in a chapter.

the packages used by the document are separately licensed by their
respective distributors. They are build prerequisites, not bundled project

AI-assisted text and diagrams must be reviewed for factual accuracy,
