## Key Areas Covered

### GDPR
- Seven principles (Art. 5): lawfulness, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, integrity, accountability
- Six legal bases (Art. 6): consent, contract, legal obligation, vital interests, public task, legitimate interests
- Data subject rights: access (15), rectification (16), erasure (17), portability (20), object (21)
- Art. 22: automated decision-making — no significant decisions solely automated without human review
- Art. 35: DPIA mandatory for high-risk processing
- Anonymisation (GDPR-free) vs pseudonymisation (GDPR still applies)
- International transfers: adequacy decisions, SCCs, BCRs; Schrems II and EU-US DPF 2023

### EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689)
- In force August 2024; world's first comprehensive horizontal AI law
- Risk tiers: Unacceptable (prohibited) / High Risk (conformity assessment) / Limited (transparency) / Minimal (no obligations)
- High-risk examples: HR/recruitment, credit scoring, medical devices, law enforcement
- Prohibited: social scoring, real-time biometric surveillance, subliminal manipulation
- High-risk obligations: risk management, data governance, logging, transparency, human oversight, accuracy/robustness
- GPAI models: documentation, TDM copyright compliance; systemic risk threshold ≥10^25 FLOPs
- EU AI Office established 2024 for oversight

### International Landscape
- US: sector-specific (FDA, CFPB, EEOC); Biden EO Oct 2023; no federal AI law
- China: Algorithm Recommendation (2022), Deep Synthesis (2022), Generative AI (2023)
- UK: pro-innovation, sector-based; AI Safety Institute 2023
- Brussels Effect: EU rules become de-facto global standards

### Intellectual Property
- Training data: EU TDM exception (DSM Directive Art. 3/4); US fair use uncertain (ongoing litigation)
- AI-generated output: generally not copyrightable (no human author) in EU and US
- Open source licences: MIT, Apache 2.0, GPL v3, LGPL, RAIL, Llama Community
- Three separate licences to check: model, training data, code

### Cybersecurity
- NIS2 (2022/2555): expanded scope, incident reporting 24h/72h, senior management liability, fines up to €10M/2% turnover
- eIDAS: simple / advanced / qualified electronic signatures

### AI Liability
- Traditional product liability: defect + damage + causation — hard with opaque ML
- Accountability gaps: opacity, distributed responsibility, autonomous behaviour, evolving systems
- EU AI Liability Directive (proposed 2022): rebuttable presumption of causality
